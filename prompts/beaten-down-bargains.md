# Beaten-down bargains

Solid, cheap, cash-rich companies that bad news knocked down 25%+ in a year.

## Find your own (paste into Claude or ChatGPT)

Act like Warren Buffett looking for bargains. Find 5 US stocks that:
1. Have a P/E ratio under 15 and real, steady profits
2. Have been in business at least 10 years and stayed profitable through hard times like 2008 and 2020
3. Have more cash than debt
4. Have dropped at least 25% in the past 52 weeks

For each one, tell me: why the price dropped (the bad news), whether that problem looks temporary or permanent, what the company does that's hard for competitors to copy (its "moat"), and the biggest risk of buying it. Use current data and tell me the date of your numbers. Then tell me which one Buffett would most likely buy and why, in plain words a teenager would understand.
